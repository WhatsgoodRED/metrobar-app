You can report a post, a comment or a score. You can block another user. Admins can hide posts, comments and scores that break these rules. The controller of your personal data is Pink Elephant Salon, Ganzenheuvel 33, 6511 WD Nijmegen, the Netherlands, ben@pinkelephantsalon.nl. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The short version. No ads. No tracking or analytics scripts. We never sell your data. Most of what you write stays in your own browser. We only store what is needed to run accounts, The Zone, sync and payments.

1. What stays on your device

  • Lyrics, songs and settings. Saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us unless you sign in and sync, or publish them.
  • Your own AI keys. If you add your own key, it is saved only in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us.
  • Recordings and imported beats. Takes you record and audio files you add are saved only in your browser (IndexedDB). They are not uploaded.
  • Microphone. The tap/BPM tools and the flow game listen to the microphone inside your browser, and only after you allow it. That sound is not recorded and not uploaded.
