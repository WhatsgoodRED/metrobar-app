Beat & tempo

Load a beat (mp3 or wav). MetroBar detects the BPM and the first beat. Press Use to take the BPM, or ½ / ×2 if it found half or double time.

No beat? Press Tap (or T) 4+ times, then keep tapping with Space. Click any tile to mark it.

Speed plays the beat and the click slower or faster; Keep pitch keeps it in tune. A loaded beat also shows its key.

Fit

Counts syllables by rule. Click a word to correct its count.

Free text? Paste it and press Split into bars.

A pickup is the one or two soft syllables sung just before beat 1.

Breakdown & Compare

Each Fit bar is drawn as a breakdown: dots for syllables, pills for held ones, the landing in red, brackets for the flow's groups.

Compare A/B/C/D shows the same words in four flows. Pick one and Practice uses it.

Practice

Puts the lines on the tiles, one per bar, with the landing slot lit.

Tap test: tick it, start Practice, and press Space (or tap the tiles) on every landing to see how many ms early or late you are. Esc stops.

Tempo trainer: starts at 70% and adds 5 BPM every 4 bars up to the beat BPM. The beat plays slower and lower while it ramps.

Rhymes & Bar heel

Rhymes colours stressed syllables that share a vowel sound within two bars.

Bar heel slides every line and its landing a 16th or 8th earlier or later: same words, different syncopation.

Pronunciation

Pronunciation dictionary off: using the spelling rules.

Syllables, stress and vowel sounds come from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (125,000 words, free). Words it does not know, and words you corrected, use the spelling rules.

Writing habit

When you open MetroBar it offers the song you were last writing, at the line where you stopped.

Only on this device. It fires while MetroBar is open in a tab or as an installed app, and only on days you have not written.

Polymeter

A cell of N steps (e.g. 7 triplet steps) repeats across the bar line, so its accents (outlined tiles, high click) land somewhere new every bar until they line up with beat 1 again.

Vocal

Load an acapella or vocal stem (load the beat first so the BPM and offset match). MetroBar finds every syllable, puts it on the grid, names each bar's flow and shows how early or late the voice is.

Paste the lyrics to see the words under the dots, then Use as bars to cut them the way they are rapped.

Studio timings at 145 BPM

Delay and reverb times locked to the tempo, for when you mix your vocal over the beat. Dotted = 1.5 times the note, triplet = 2/3.

Reverb that ends on the beat

Move to another tempo
Land every bar. Three steps to drill a verse on the beat:
  1. Load a beat (mp3 or wav). MetroBar finds the tempo; press Use. No beat? Tap the tempo with Tap.
  2. Paste your lyrics in Fit, one line per bar, and press Fit. You see syllables, flow and where each bar lands.
  3. Press Practice, then Start (or Space). Your words run over the tiles at tempo; the red tile is the landing.
