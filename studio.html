Practice only. This is the site's own player. Rap over it and record takes, but its beat can't be mixed into your song or exported. Only your vocal takes export (Export vocals). Press play in the player, then Record.

Use headphones, so the beat doesn't bleed into your take.

Space = play/stop · R = record · click the ruler or a lane to move the playhead, drag on the ruler to set a loop · drag a take to move it, its edges to trim · FX opens a take's gain, high-pass, compressor, reverb and fades.
